Detailed Description

Whether the fleet's heartbeat is still heard, with a timeout.

States are those of CapabilityStatus.LINK_*: NOT_MONITORED with a timeout of 0 (or less), WAITING until the first heartbeat, then OK or LOST depending on the age of the last one. Thread-safe.
